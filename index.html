How to use it

Open a subject, pick a topic, read the summary, then test yourself with the key-term flashcards and practice questions. Tick “Mark as revised” to track your progress (saved in your browser only).

Syllabus-first

Every subject follows the current IB subject guide. Assessment weightings and topic lists are on each subject's overview page. Always check with your teacher for your school's options.
